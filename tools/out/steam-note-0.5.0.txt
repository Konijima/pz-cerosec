[b]0.5.0 - The building answers back[/b]
Safe for existing saves. A generator you already controlled needs a cable now.

[b]New[/b]
[*] Cables reach fixtures a computer cannot otherwise touch. Right-click a fixture that has a module and take [b]Link to computer[/b]: a lamppost, a gate, the shop across the street.
[*] One Electric Wire per tile, up to thirty tiles, and unlinking gives the wire back. Move the computer and the cable stays on the fixture: [b]Unlink loose cable[/b] takes it off.
[*] Wiring or unwiring a door, window or curtain needs the same access as fitting its module: open, drawn, or from inside.
[*] One word now works a whole kind of device at once: [b]dev window close[/b] shuts every window, [b]dev light off[/b] kills every light.
[*] A program left running with [b]&[/b] is still running when you reload your save.
[*] The power switch is mechanical now: leave it on and the computer restarts itself when the power returns. [b]halt[/b] or [b]shutdown[/b] keeps it off.
[*] You can hear the building work: doors, windows and curtains it moves make their sound for everyone in the room.
[*] A module comes off with its fixture. Pick it up, sledge it, let a zombie break it in: the module ends up on the floor.
[*] Works beside the Workshop mod Computer Mod: whichever mod switches a desktop on keeps it until it is switched off.
[*] A generator needs a cable now. Fit a Generator Switch and take [b]Link to computer[/b], the same as a lamppost: standing beside it is no longer enough.

[b]Fixed[/b]
[*] Standing outside a house no longer reaches anything inside it: devices, network, radio and motion sensors all stop at the wall.
[*] Doors, windows, curtains and lamps on the south or east wall were invisible to /dev. All are found now; your device numbers do not change.
[*] A porch lamp can take a relay from the sidewalk. The inside-only rule now applies to doors, windows and curtains, not to lamps.
[*] Every machine has [b]/usr/local/bin[/b] on its PATH, so [b]curtains.sh auto[/b] runs by name the way the HOME disk's README says.
[*] The device table columns line up again: [b]curtain0[/b] no longer runs into the room name beside it.
[*] Dropped modules all look like the same small circuit board, never scrap metal.
[*] A long line piped, redirected or captured with [b]$( )[/b] is no longer cut at 60 characters. The screen itself is unchanged.
[*] Tooltips on CeroSec's menu entries no longer run two words together at the line break.

[b]Servers[/b]
[*] With [b]Safehouse members only[/b] on, a stranger can no longer cable a claimed house's doors or windows.
[*] The debug window is back for server admins: [b]CeroSec (dev)[/b] at the bottom of a computer's menu.

[b]Interface[/b]
[*] One CeroSec entry per fixture in the right-click menu, now after the fixture's own options, with a small green terminal icon. Hovering a line highlights the right object.
[*] A greyed-out menu line says why, in red.
[*] The CeroSec entry only shows up when there's something you can actually do with it.
